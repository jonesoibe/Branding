About

I keep payment systems alive. Every card tap, transfer and withdrawal passes through a front-end processor, and I'm the engineer who makes sure it keeps answering.

I'm a results-oriented technical support specialist with deep experience in Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) ecosystems and Postilion FEP administration. I ensure high availability of payment infrastructure through proactive monitoring and rigorous Tier 2 support.

I manage integrations with the major payment schemes (Visa, MasterCard, Verve), drive PCI-DSS compliance, and automate workflows with SQL and PowerShell. I work across teams to roll out new card products and squeeze more performance out of transaction processing.
